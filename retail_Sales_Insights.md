## Key Insights & Recommendations (Question 14)

**Tools:** PostgreSQL (pgAdmin) | **Tables:** customers, orders, order_items, products, payments

### Snapshot

| Metric | Result |
|---|---|
| Customers | 1,000 |
| Orders | 5,000 |
| Total revenue (2025 + 2026) | 2,517,303,000 |
| Average order value | ~502,000 – 504,000 (stable across both years) |
| Pending payments | 206,656,000 (about 8% of revenue) |
| Customers who never ordered | 7 (0.7%) |

---

### 1. Sales Insights

- **Revenue is healthy and the average order value is steady.** 2025 generated 1,457,203,500 and 2026 has generated 1,060,099,500 so far. Average order value barely moved (504,397 in 2025 vs 502,179 in 2026), so revenue changes are driven by the number of orders, not by bigger or smaller baskets.
- **Revenue is concentrated in a few products.** Laptop is the top product at 673.2M (about 27% of all revenue). Laptop, Standing Desk and Monitor together contribute about 52% of revenue.
- **Electronics and Furniture carry the business.** Electronics earns 1.12B (44.5%) and Furniture 716.6M (28.5%), together about 73% of revenue. Software adds 21.4%, while Accessories (4.5%) and Stationery (1.1%) contribute very little.
- **Top-selling by volume is not top-earning by value.** USB Hub (1,653 units), Planner (1,590) and Notebook (1,576) lead on units but belong to low-revenue categories. Laptop leads revenue without appearing in the top 7 by units. Monitor, Accounting Software and Bookshelf rank highly on both lists, making them the most reliable all-round performers.
- **Revenue is spread across cities, with no single dominant market.** Abeokuta leads (312.2M, about 12%), followed by Enugu (274.7M) and Port Harcourt (273.9M). The top 8 cities all fall between 230M and 312M.
- **Monthly revenue fluctuates.** In the first half of 2025, monthly revenue ranged from 94.6M in February to 134.9M in March.

### 2. Customer Insights

- **Customers are loyal.** 964 of the 993 customers who have ordered (97%) placed more than one order, averaging about 5 orders each, with the most active placing 12.
- **There is no heavy dependence on a few customers.** The top spender (Ngozi Obi, 9.47M) accounts for under 0.4% of total revenue. Average spend per customer is about 2.54M, and the top customer spends roughly 3.7 times that.
- **A large group of high-value customers exists.** 442 customers (about 44.5%) spend above the average.
- **Almost everyone has bought.** Only 7 of 1,000 customers have never placed an order.

### 3. Payment Insights

- **Payment methods are used evenly.** Paid amounts are similar across Bank Transfer (471.6M), Card (454.9M) and Cash (456.3M), so no single method is a point of risk.
- **206.7M is tied up in pending payments**, roughly 8% of total revenue. Bank Transfer, Card and Cash each have 87-92 pending payments, so the delay is not specific to one method.
- **Refunds are recorded without a value.** Refunded payments (for example 32 for Bank Transfer and 33 for Card) all show an amount of 0.00, so the true financial impact of refunds cannot be measured from this data.

---

### Recommendations

1. **Recover pending payments.** Introduce automated payment reminders and clear due dates to turn the 206.7M in pending payments into cash.
2. **Protect and grow the core categories.** Keep Electronics and Furniture well stocked, and bundle low-price accessories and stationery with laptops and desks to raise order value.
3. **Reward loyalty.** With 97% of customers already repeat buyers, a loyalty or rewards programme aimed at the 442 above-average spenders could lift revenue further.
4. **Re-engage inactive customers.** Run a targeted first-purchase offer for the 7 customers who have never ordered.
5. **Improve refund tracking.** Record the refunded amount so the cost of refunds can be analysed.
6. **Look into seasonality.** Check whether the February dip seen in 2025 repeats in 2026, and plan promotions around slow months.
7. **Learn from top cities.** Study what is working in Abeokuta, Enugu and Port Harcourt and test it in the lowest-performing cities.

### Data Notes

- 2026 is a partial year, so 2025 and 2026 totals should not be compared directly.
- Refunded payments carry an amount of 0.00, which limits refund analysis.
